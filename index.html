Agile

What is Agile?

Agile is a mindset, not a process. It is a set of values and principles that guide how teams build software iteratively and collaboratively.

Rather than planning everything upfront and delivering at the end, Agile teams deliver in small, working increments, adapting to feedback continuously.

Born from the Agile Manifesto (2001), when 17 developers signed it in Snowbird, Utah.

Comparison

Agile vs Waterfall

Dimension Waterfall Agile
Delivery Single big release at the end Continuous, incremental releases
Planning All upfront (fixed scope) Adaptive, sprint-by-sprint
Change Expensive & discouraged Welcomed at any stage
Testing Phase at the end Every sprint / continuous
Customer At start & end only Ongoing collaboration
Teams Siloed by function Cross-functional & self-organising
Risk Discovered late Surfaced early each sprint

Definition of Done (DoD)

A shared team agreement on what "complete" really means. If it's not met, it doesn't ship.

Jira

What is Jira?

Jira is Atlassian's project tracking tool built for Agile teams. It's where Scrum artifacts live digitally.

Think of Jira as the execution layer: Agile gives you principles, Scrum gives you the framework, and Jira gives you the board, tickets, and visibility.
